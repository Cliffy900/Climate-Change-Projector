config.py: Holds all settings, constants, and hyperparameters in one place.

data_loader.py: Exclusively handles reading raw data (I/O).

data_splitter.py: Exclusively handles splitting the data into training and testing sets.

feature_engineer.py: Exclusively handles transformations, scaling, and feature creation.

trainer.py: Initializes and fits the model.

persister.py: Handles saving the trained model and the preprocessing tools (like Scalers).

main.py: The central execution script that orchestrates the entire workflow.
